# 📘 Feature: Plan Your Next Day/Week/Month/Year

**Feature ID:** LOA-F001  
**Feature Name:** Intelligent Planning Assistant  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Priority:** High  
**Author:** Yasir Imran  
**Date:** 06/11/2025  

---

## 🧠 Inspiration (from _Getting Things Done_ by David Allen)
**Chapter Reference:** Capture – Collect everything that grabs your attention (tasks, ideas, reminders) in an external system (like a notebook or app).  

### Problem  
Users often struggle with lack of focus and productivity because they don’t have a reliable system to manage their thoughts, tasks, and goals.

### Solution  
Create a structured “system” that captures tasks, priorities, and ideas. The app will act as a *process guide*, helping users focus by planning their next day, week, month, or year through interaction with an AI assistant.  

---

## 🧩 Feature Description  
A gentle, conversational planning session with an **AI personal assistant** that helps users:  
- Reflect on previous tasks and accomplishments.  
- Capture new goals and tasks for upcoming time periods.  
- Organize them based on importance, urgency, and long-term objectives.  
- Schedule and prioritize automatically.  

The experience should feel like interacting with a real-life **Personal Assistant (PA)** who guides users step-by-step in creating actionable plans.

---

## 💬 User Interaction Flow
1. **User opens the “Plan Ahead” feature.**  
2. **AI Assistant initiates conversation** – e.g., “Would you like to plan your next day or week?”  
3. **User responds naturally** (text or voice).  
4. **AI captures goals/tasks** and categorizes them (work, personal, study, etc.).  
5. **AI suggests an optimized schedule** and checks if the user wants reminders or changes.  
6. **Final confirmation** – plan is saved and synced across user’s calendar or dashboard.
